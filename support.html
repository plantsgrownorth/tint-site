Help and support

Something not working, or have an idea? Here is the quickest way to get it fixed.

Quick fixes

  • Reload the YouTube tab. Most changes apply instantly, but a reload clears anything stuck.
  • Open Tint's settings, go to Help, and turn on Safe mode. If the problem goes away, Tint caused it. Turn Safe mode off again afterwards.
  • YouTube changes its page often. Tint picks up fixes on its own within a few hours.

Report a problem

In Tint's settings, go to Help and click Copy report. It copies your Tint version and settings, never your history. Then open a new issue, describe what happened, and paste the report.

Take the tour again

Settings, then Help, then Start the tour. It walks through everything Tint does in about a minute.

Tint is not made by or connected to YouTube or Google.
